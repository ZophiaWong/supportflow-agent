# Account Unlock Guide

If an administrator is locked out after a password reset, confirm the latest reset email was used, verify the organization name, and unlock the account before forcing another reset.

If the account is still locked, support should collect the affected email address, the time of the failed login, and whether multifactor authentication blocked access.
