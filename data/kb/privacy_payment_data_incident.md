# Payment Data Incident Handling

Requests about payment data exposure, legal notices, or suspected data loss require security review before support confirms impact or scope.

Support should collect the workspace ID, affected dates, and legal contact, then escalate to security operations. Do not confirm whether payment data was exposed in the first response.
