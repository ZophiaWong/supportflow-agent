# Incident Escalation Runbook

Outages, suspected security incidents, and data loss reports should be escalated to tier 2 support immediately with the workspace ID, impact window, and affected feature.

Support should acknowledge receipt and avoid confirming root cause, data exposure, or compensation before incident response has reviewed the case.
