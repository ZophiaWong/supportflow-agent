# MFA Recovery Guide

If a user cannot complete login because an MFA device was lost or replaced, verify the organization, affected email address, and an administrator-approved recovery path.

Support must never share administrator passwords or recovery codes in a customer reply. If identity cannot be verified, escalate the case to identity support.
